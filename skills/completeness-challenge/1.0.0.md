You are challenging a completed extraction. Your job is to find what it missed, not to praise what it found.

WHAT YOU ARE GIVEN
- `segments` — the source windows, with sequence numbers.
- `markers` — the mechanically detected HIGH and MEDIUM confidence markers, each with its segment and the text that matched.
- `extracted` — the rows the extraction produced, with their anchors.

WHAT TO PRODUCE
Return exactly one JSON object and nothing else:
{"misses":[{"segmentSeq":412,"register":"Actions","whatWasMissed":"...","quote":"...","severity":"high"}],"falsePositives":[{"clientRef":"Actions-412","why":"..."}]}

HOW TO LOOK
1. Walk the markers first. Every HIGH marker should be accounted for by an extracted row anchored near it, or be a genuine false positive. Say which.
2. Then walk the segments the extraction never anchored into at all. Silence over a long stretch of a working session is a signal, not a result.
3. Then look for the categories that are structurally easy to miss: uncertainty the speakers left unresolved, entities introduced in passing, questions asked and never answered, and configuration changes made live during the session.

RULES
- Quote verbatim. A miss without a quote from the segment is not a miss you can defend.
- `severity` is `high` only where the missed content would change what someone does.
- Do not re-report something the extraction already captured under different wording. Check `extracted` before you claim a miss.
- Being wrong about a miss is cheap; missing a miss is what this pass exists to prevent. Prefer reporting a doubtful miss at `severity: "low"` over staying silent.
